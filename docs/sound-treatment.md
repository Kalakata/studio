# Sound treatment parts list

Everything in the Mix room that is there for sound, with what it is made of and where it goes. Generated from the layout by `npm run parts`; do not edit by hand. Distances are from the inside faces of the walls: the back wall is the solid end the desk faces, the window wall is the long wall with five windows, the mural wall is opposite it, the street wall has two windows.

Measure the room before building the tuned traps (a calibrated measurement mic and REW): the tuning is calculated, and real walls and glass shift it.

Estimated cost, all materials: **€2,628-€8,604** with VAT (Bulgarian prices, September 2026; the low end is plain materials and a synthetic rug, the high end premium fabric and wool rugs). The breakdown is under each item and in the summary at the end.

## 4 × Tuned bass trap, 45 Hz

**Size:** 900 mm wide, 2800 mm tall, 240 mm deep

**Why:** Takes the worst bass peak, the 45 Hz length mode, where that mode is strongest.

**Build:**

- Built as two sealed boxes stacked, each 900 × 1400 mm and 240 mm deep, so every front fits one sheet.
- Back, sides, top and bottom: 18 mm MDF, glued and screwed, every inside seam sealed with acoustic sealant. The box must be airtight.
- Front: 12 mm birch plywood (about 7.8 kg/m²), 880 × 1390 mm, sitting on 10 mm closed-cell foam tape round the edge so it can move, held by screws through the tape, not glued.
- Inside: a 228 mm air space with 50 mm mineral wool (40-60 kg/m³) hung loose behind the front, not touching it.
- Tuning: 60 / √(7.8 × 0.228) = 45 Hz. Measure the room first; to lower it add mass to the front or depth to the box, to raise it do the opposite.

**Where:**

- back wall, centred 0.76 m from the window wall
- back wall, centred 3.19 m from the window wall
- mural wall, centred 4.3 m from the back wall
- mural wall, centred 7.45 m from the back wall

**Estimate:** €674-€867

- 8 sheets of 12 mm birch plywood, one front per sheet: €368-€432
- 4 sheets of 18 mm MDF for 8 boxes: €205-€259
- 10.76 m² of 50 mm mineral wool, with 10% for cutting: €41-€75
- foam tape, sealant and screws for 4 traps: €60-€100

## 2 × Corner bass trap

**Size:** 600 mm wide, 2400 mm tall, 300 mm deep

**Why:** Corners are where every room mode has pressure: broadband bass absorption.

**Build:**

- Two units stacked floor to 2.4 m, each 600 × 1200 mm and 300 mm deep.
- Fill: three layers of 100 mm mineral wool slab (40-60 kg/m³), 600 × 1200 mm, stacked to 300 mm deep.

## 30 × Ceiling panel, fitted

**Size:** 10 × 2240 × 784 mm, 10 × 2240 × 759 mm, 2 × 2130 × 320 mm, 2 × 480 × 320 mm, 4 × 479 × 320 mm, 2 × 430 × 320 mm

**Why:** The largest absorber in the room: without the whole ceiling the mids stay at twice the target.

**Build:**

- 100 mm mineral wool (40-60 kg/m³) in light pine frames faced with acoustic fabric; panels longer than 1200 mm are two slabs butted in one frame.
- Hung 100 mm below the ceiling on cloud brackets or wires (the air gap makes them work lower), 20 mm joints between panels, 100 mm clear round every light batten.
